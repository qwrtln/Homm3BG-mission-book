\textbf{Author:} BoberL

\textit{This scenario is made for competetive play. It requires careful budgeting and trade in order to allow for the most equal and unique gameplay.} % no-check-caps

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}

8, 10 or 12 Rounds

\textit{Scenario length is based on Map Generator Size (S, M or L)}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}

\textbf{Player Count:} 2

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 12 \svg{gold}, 3 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 15 \svg{gold}, 2 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Search~(2) the \svg{bronze} Neutral Deck. Do this after completing the \textbf{Setup Changes} mentioned below.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze}~Dwelling, Citadel

\textbf{Map Tile Pool:} Far (II--III) Map Tile. This Tile serves as \textbf{the Bunker}.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}

Take the Map Tiles according to the generated layout. Choose your available expansions and desired map size in the settings.

\textbf{https://zedero.github.io/homm3boardgame}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Eliminate all opponents.
\end{itemize}
If the condition isn't met, immidiately start the final Combat at the end of the last Round.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}

\textbf{\nth{4} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Remove all Black Cubes from the map
  \item Each hero gains 1 \svg{movement}.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\nth{7} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Repeat the Events from Round 4.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\nth{11} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Repeat the Events from Round 4.
\end{itemize}
\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}

\subsubsection*{Setup Changes}
\begin{itemize}
  \item This scenario requires Players to \textbf{Trade} to establish the game setup. This is explained in the rules below.
  \item This scenario cannot be played on Easy Difficulty.
  \item Reroll the map generation if a Center (VI-VII) and Starting (I) Tiles are adjacent.
  \item Split the \svg{artifact} Deck by rarity into 3 separate Decks: \textbf{Relic}, \textbf{Major}, and \textbf{Minor}.
  \item Split the \svg{spellpower} Deck by strength into 2 separate Decks: \textbf{Basic}, \textbf{Expert}.
\end{itemize}

\columnbreak

\subsubsection*{Trade Rules}
Before the game, players must \textbf{Trade} according to the following steps. Establish which Player starts the \textbf{Trade} by throwing 2 Attack Dice each. The player with a higher sum starts all the following actions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each Player may remove a Faction of their choice from the pool once.
  \item Choose 2 Factions randomly. Players may reroll the result for free, once each. Players may \svg{pay} their opponent 1~\svg{gold}, to do this again, once.
  \item \textbf{Bidding 1:} The winner gets to their starting Faction from the 2.
  \item \textbf{Bidding 2:} The winner gets to choose their starting position.
  \item \textbf{Bidding 3:} The winner gets to choose whether they move first or second during Rounds.
  \item Both Players draw the \textbf{Starting Units}. The other Player may \svg{pay} 2~\svg{gold} to have their opponent redo the \textbf{Search} action.
\end{itemize}
After completing the \textbf{Trade}, at the end of Round 1, Players decide if they want to \textbf{Keep} or \textbf{Reset} the current setup. The Player whose turn goes first, must state their intentions first.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Keep}: The Player states intention to keep the current map setup. If both Players \textbf{Keep}, the game goes on as normal.
  \item \textbf{Reset}: The Player decides to reset the current map generation. Do not \textbf{Trade} again if this happens. This action can be done only once each by \svg{pay} 2~\svg{gold} to the opponent.
\end{itemize}

\vspace*{\fill}
\columnbreak

\subsubsection*{Bunker Rules}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Fights on \textbf{The Bunker} tile are one difficulty lower. Place it next to the Starting (I) Tile and uncover it.
  \item Fights in \textbf{The Bunker} cannot give more than 1 \svg{experience}.
  \item Ending a turn in \textbf{The Bunker} grants 1 extra \svg{movement} for the next Round.
  \item You may only enter and exit \textbf{The Bunker} through the Starting (I) Tile.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Break Rules}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A Player begins Combat against the \textbf{Guard} when attempting to uncover a Near (IV-V) or Center (VI-VII) Tile for the first time.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Guard} is a Tier IV army with one Neutral \svg{silver} Unit being replaced with a \svg{golden} Unit.
    \item After defeating the \textbf{Guard}, gain 2 \svg{experience}.
  \end{itemize}
  \item A Player that fails to defeat the \textbf{Guard} ends their turn at the spot they tried to Break from and doesn't uncover the Tile.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Gameplay Changes}

\begin{itemize}
  \item When you Search the \svg{artifact} Deck, roll 2 Attack Dice to determine which \svg{artifact} Deck to draw from.
  \item You may only gain Expert \svg{spellpower} if any of your Heroes is standing on Near (IV-V) and Center (VI-VII) Tiles.
  \item Field Rewards vary depending on Difficulty according to the table below.
  \item \textbf{Upgrade:} When performing \textbf{Search} actions from Field Rewards, you may turn any attack Die of your choice to +1.
  \item After defeating a Random Town Tile, Players may \textbf{Recruit} and \textbf{Reinforce} Units fought. Increase \svg{gold}, \svg{building_materials} or \svg{valuables} income accordingly.
  \item The Grail serves as a \svg{permanent} Card that doesn't count into the limit.
\end{itemize}
