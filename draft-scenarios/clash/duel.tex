\textit{This scenario is made for competetive play. It requires careful budgeting and trade in order to allow for the most equal and unique gameplay.} % no-check-caps

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}

8 Rounds

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}

\textbf{Player Count:} 2

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 10 \svg{gold}, 3 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 15 \svg{gold}, 2 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Search~(2) the \svg{bronze} Neutral Deck. Do this after completing the \textbf{Setup Changes} mentioned below.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze}~Dwelling, Citadel

\textbf{Map Tile Pool:} Each player takes 1 random Far (II--III) Map Tile. This Tile serves as the \textbf{Bunker}.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}

Take the Map Tiles according to the generated layout.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Obtain the Grail Token and bring it to
your Faction Town, OR
  \item Eliminate all opponents.
\end{itemize}
If neither condition is met, the Players immidiately start the final Combat at the end of Round 8.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}

\textbf{\nth{3} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Remove all Black Cubes from the map
  \item Gain 1 \svg{movement}
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\nth{6} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Repeat the Timed Events of Round 3.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}

\subsubsection*{Setup Changes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Before each playthrough of this Scenario, players must \textbf{Trade} according to the following steps:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Establish which Player starts the \textbf{Trade} by throwing 2 Attack Dice.
      \item The starting Player starts bidding on which position on the Map will they begin at. The bidding ends when one of the Players grabs both \svg{gold} Piles from the table instead of bidding up.
      \item Next, the starting Player starts bidding to be the first to start the Round.
      \item Next, the starting Player draws the Starting Army according to \textbf{Starting Units}. The opposing Player may \svg{pay} 2~\svg{gold} to have their opponent redo the \textbf{Search} action. This can be done once. After the starting Player finishes his starting army draft, do the same, with the roles reversed.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Bunker} serves as a lower Difficulty Tile. \textit{Example: The scenario is played out on Hard Difficulty, all Combat in the Bunker are played out as if they were in Normal Difficulty}
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Break Rules}
\begin{itemize}
  \item When attempting to uncover their first (IV-V) or (VI-VII) Tile, before seeing the Tiles content, the Player begins Combat agains the \textbf{Guard}. % maybe there's a better way to phrase that
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf The {Guard} is a Tier IV army with one Neutral \svg{silver} Unit being replaced with a \svg{golden} Unit.
    \item After defeating the \textbf{Guard}, gain 2 \svg{experience}.
  \end{itemize}
  \item If the Player fails to defeat the \textbf{Guard}, they stay at their spot and doesn't uncover the Tile they tried to Break into.
\end{itemize}
