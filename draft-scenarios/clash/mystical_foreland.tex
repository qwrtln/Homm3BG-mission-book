\textbf{Author:} BoberL

\textit{Colonization of the unseen lands went well, until your scouts brought news of unknown horse trails by the nearby lake.
You are not the only one looking for the Mystical Foreland.
Double the nightguard and move out at dawn.}  % no-check-caps

%This Scenario requires the Conflux, Cove \textit{(or Naval Battles for Tile variety)}, Stronghold \textit{(or Stretch Goals Regular for Tile variety)}, Inferno, and Rampart Expansions.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}
14 Rounds

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}
\textbf{Player Count:} 4

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 13~\svg{gold}, 3~\svg{building_materials}, 1~\svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 10~\svg{gold}, 0~\svg{building_materials}, 0~\svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Search~(2) the \svg{bronze} Neutral Deck
  \item  A ,,Few'' chose \svg{bronze} Units
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze} Dwelling

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}
Take the following Map Tiles and arrange them as shown in the Scenario map layout:

\begin{itemize}
  \item 4 × Starting (I) Map Tiles
  \item 8 × Far (II--III) Map Tiles
  \item 10 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles (2 Land, 6 Sea, 2 Subterranean). These Tiles should not contain any Obelisks.
  \item 3 × Center (VI--VII) Map Tiles (1 Grail, 1 Sea, 1 Subterranean)
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}
Obtain the Grail Token and bring it to your Faction Town.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}
\textbf{\nth{4} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each player may immediately \svg{reinforce} one \svg{bronze} Unit of their choice for free, or one \svg{silver} Unit of their choice at a cost reduced by 4~\svg{gold}.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{6} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Remove all Black Cubes from the map except those on Learning Stones.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{9} Round:}
\begin{itemize}	
  \item Resolve an Event card twice.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{10} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item From now on, Neutral \svg{golden} Units gain an extra \textbf{Stack Token}, except when fighting the \textbf{Grail Army} or another player.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{12} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each player may \svg{pay} 5~\svg{gold} to Search~(2) the \svg{artifact}, \svg{skill}, or \svg{spellpower} Deck.
    A player may do this up to 3 times, paying 5~\svg{gold} each time.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}

\textbf{Setup changes:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item A Hero may not move between a \textcolor{red}{\textbf{Red Tile}} and a Sea Tile.
  \item \textcolor{blue}{\textbf{Blue Tiles}} are the Subterranean Tiles.
  \item Tiles that have the \textcolor{inferno}{\textbf{Red Cubes}} contain \textbf{Creature Banks}.
  \item Before the game, remove the \textbf{Diplomacy} Ability, the \textbf{Den of Thieves} and \textbf{Prison} Events, and the \textbf{Ambassador's Sash} and \textbf{Diplomat's Ring} Artifacts.
  \item Level VII Neutral Combats cannot be skipped.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Grail rules:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textcolor{rampart}{\textbf{Lime Tile}} must contain the Grail.
  \item To conquer the Grail (VII), players fight the \textbf{Grail Army} instead of the regular Neutral army.
    It contains the regular Neutral Units plus an extra 1~\svg{azure} Unit and 1~\svg{golden} Unit.
    Each enemy Unit gets a permanent \textbf{Stack Token}.
  \item If a player obtains the Grail in the \nth{13} or \nth{14} Round. The Scenario ends after 2 Rounds.
  \item A player who is defeated in Combat or Surrenders drops the Grail Token on the Field where the Hero was defeated.
  \item A player holding the Grail Token has their \svg{hand} Limit increased by 2.
  \item If a Hero holding the Grail Token uses Dimension Door, Fly, or Town Portal, the Grail Token is placed on the Field the Hero occupied before the move.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Neutral Unit Recruitment:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Once at any point during your Turn, you may \svg{pay} one of the following to Search~(2) the Neutral Units Deck:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item 3~\svg{gold}, 1~\svg{building_materials} for \svg{bronze}
      \item 5~\svg{gold}, 2~\svg{building_materials}, 1~\svg{valuables} for \svg{silver}
      \item 9~\svg{gold}, 4~\svg{building_materials}, 2~\svg{valuables} for \svg{golden}
      \item 15~\svg{gold}, 6~\svg{building_materials}, 4~\svg{valuables} for \svg{azure}
    \end{itemize}
    You may then \svg{recruit} one of the Units you found for its full cost.
\end{itemize}

\columnbreak

\textbf{Field Rewards:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Sea Temple}: 15~\svg{gold}.
    Search~(3) the \svg{artifact} Deck \textbf{twice}.
    You may replace 2 Statistic Cards from your Deck or Discard with their upgraded versions, then take both upgraded Cards into your hand.
  \item \textbf{Level VII Town:} You may \svg{recruit} and \svg{reinforce} one of the Units from the defending Town for half its cost, excluding \svg{valuables} (rounded down). \textit{Example: After defeating a Castle Army, you may now \svg{recruit} and \svg{reinforce} Archangels for 25~\svg{gold} and 3~\svg{valuables}}
  \item \textbf{Cyclops Stockpile}: 10~\svg{building_materials}, 5~\svg{valuables}. Your Units gain a permanent +1~\svg{attack} and +1~\svg{initiative}, including Units you Recruit later.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Map Constraints:}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \svg{golden} Neutral Units fought during and after \textbf{Round 10} gain an extra \textbf{Stack token}. Ignore this rule, when fighting the \textbf{Grail Army} or other players.
  \item In a 2v2 Scenario, each team places one player on a \textcolor{red}{red-outlined} Starting (I) Tile and one player on a Starting (I) Tile that is not outlined.
\end{itemize}
