\begin{multicols*}{2}

\textbf{Author:} Laamakala

\textit{"The fabric of reality ripples, tearing open unstable pathways across the islands. Scholars call it a localized temporal anomaly, but to the Lords of War, it is simply a playground of swift violence. Step through the rifts, claim the rogue magic of Pandora's boxes, and embrace the beautiful chaos of the portal network."}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}
This Scenario plays out over 10 Rounds.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}
\textbf{Player Count:} 2--6

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 5 \svg{gold}, 6 \svg{building_materials}, 4 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 15 \svg{gold}, 4 \svg{building_materials}, 3 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 3 × \svg{bronze} Units
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze} Dwelling

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}

Take the following Map Tiles and arrange them as shown in the Scenario map layout ($P$ stands for the number of players):

\begin{itemize}
  \item $P$ × Starting (I) Map Tiles
  \item $P$ × Far (II--III) Map Tiles with Settlements
  \item $P$+3 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}
The game ends when any of the following conditions are met:

\begin{itemize}
  \item One player has defeated every opponent's Main Hero or captured their Starting Town at least once. \textit{That player wins the game immediately.}
  \item At the end of Round 10.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Points}}
If no player has achieved an immediate victory, the player with the most Victory Points (VP) wins.

\begin{itemize}
  \item 1 VP for the first player to use a portal.
  \item 2 VPs for each enemy Main Hero defeated.
  \item 2 VPs for each enemy Town captured.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}
\textbf{\nth{1} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Gain an Empowered Defense Statistic Card.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{2} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Search(3) any Deck.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{3} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Search(3) any Deck and gain a Spell Scroll.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{5}, \nth{6} \& \nth{7} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The Player(s) with least \svg{experience}, gain 1 \svg{experience}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Gain 5 \svg{gold} and draw Cards up to your \svg{hand} Limit after defeating a Neutral Combat.
  \item Each player may build Mage Guild at any time for free without spending Building Token.
  \item City Hall and Special Buildings can be built for half price. (Cost in \svg{gold} Rounded up; \svg{building_materials} and \svg{valuables} are rounded down.)
  \item After capturing a Settlement, that Player may also recruit Units of that Settlement's Faction when using Population Token. If that Faction is already in play, randomly choose one unused Faction instead.
  \item Players may remove one Black Cube from the Map at the start of their turn.
  \item After a Combat against another Players Main Hero:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item The winner gains 2 \svg{experience}; the loser gains 1 \svg{experience}.
    \item Draw Cards up to your \svg{hand} Limit.
    \item Gain \svg{gold} equal to half of the value of your defeated Units Rounded down.
  \end{itemize}
  \item When revealing a Near Map Tile or using a Portal for the first time, gain a Pandora's Box Card.
  \item All Neutral Combats in the Center Island are considered +1 level.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Portals}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each Town acts as a two-way Portal linked to that Island's Near Tile Portal.
  \item Each Near Tile has a two-way Portallocated on its Blocked Field. This Portal becomes active only after all Neutral Combats on that tile have been defeated.
  \item The Center Island features Portals in each Tile's Blocked Field.
  \item When teleporting to Center Island, assign one of the Resources types to each Map Tile, then roll a Resource Die to determine which Map Tile you teleport to.
  \item Using a portal costs 1 \svg{movement}.
\end{itemize}
