\begin{multicols*}{2}

\textbf{Author:} Laamakala

\textit{The fabric of reality ripples, tearing open unstable pathways across the islands. Scholars call it a localized temporal anomaly, but to the Lords of War, it is simply a playground of swift violence. Step through the rifts, claim the rogue magic of Pandora's Boxes, and embrace the beautiful chaos of the portal network.}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}
This Scenario plays out over 10 Rounds.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}
\textbf{Player Count:} 2--6

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 5 \svg{gold}, 6 \svg{building_materials}, 4 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 15 \svg{gold}, 4 \svg{building_materials}, 3 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 3 × A ``Few'' of \svg{bronze} Units
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze} Dwelling

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}

Take the following Map Tiles and arrange them as shown in the Scenario map layout ($P$ stands for the number of players):

\begin{itemize}
  \item $P$ × Starting (I) Map Tiles
  \item $P$ × Far (II--III) Map Tiles with Settlements of unused Factions
  \item $P$+1 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles for 2--3 players
  \item $P$+2 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles for 4 players
  \item $P$+3 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles for 5--6 players
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}
The game ends when any of the following conditions are met:

\begin{itemize}
  \item One player has defeated or captured every opponent's Main Hero or Starting Town at least once. \textit{That player wins the game immediately.}
  \item At the end of Round 10.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Points}}
If no player has achieved an immediate victory, the player with the most Victory Points (VP) wins.

\begin{itemize}
  \item 1 VP for the first player to use a portal.
  \item 2 VPs for each enemy Main Hero defeated.
  \item 2 VPs for each enemy Town captured.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}
\textbf{\nth{1} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item All players may gain an Empowered Defense Statistic Card.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{2} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Search (3) \svg{artifact}, \svg{skill}, or \svg{spellpower}.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{3} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Search (3) \svg{artifact}, \svg{skill}, \svg{spellpower}, or your Deck, \textit{and} gain a Spell Scroll.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{5}, \nth{6} \& \nth{7} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The player(s) with least \svg{experience}, gain 1 \svg{experience}.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{8} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item All players may Recruit and Reinforce one \svg{silver} Unit for free.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Players may use their Build Token to exchange Resources using the following rates:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item 3 \svg{gold} \svg{arrows_gray} 2 \svg{building_materials} \svg{arrows_gray} 1 \svg{valuables}
  \end{itemize}
  \item Once per Round, during your turn, you may discard 1 \svg{valuables} to draw 2 Cards.
  \item Each player may build Mage Guild at any time for free without spending their Build Token.
  \item City Hall and Special Buildings can be built for half price. (Cost in \svg{gold} is Rounded up; \svg{building_materials} and \svg{valuables} are rounded down.)
  \item Gain 5 \svg{gold} and draw Cards up to your \svg{hand} Limit after winning a Neutral Combat.
  \item \textbf{Settlement:} Add its Faction Units to your accessible Units.
  \item \textbf{Near Tiles \& Portals:} When revealing a Near Map Tile, gain a Pandora's Box Card and place a Portal to the Blocked Field on that Tile.
  \item When using a Portal for the first time, gain a Pandora's Box Card.
  \item All IV--VI Neutral Combats in the Center Island are considered +1 Level.
  \item You may use Center (VI--VII) Map Tiles on the Center Island.
  \item Players may remove 1 Black Cube from the Center Island at the start of their turn.
  \item \textbf{PvP Combat:} After a Combat against another player's Main Hero:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item The winner: gain 2 \svg{experience} \& Search (4) \svg{artifact}
    \item The loser: gain 1 \svg{experience} \& an Empowered Token.
    \item Both players draw Cards up to their \svg{hand} Limit.
    \item Both players gain \svg{gold} equal to half the total value of their defeated Units Rounded down.
  \end{itemize}
  \item You may teleport your Main Hero to defend your Town by discarding 2 Cards.
  \item After capturing an enemy Town, increase all incomes by one space.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Portals}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each Town acts as a two-way Portal linked to that Island's Near Tile Portal.
  \item Each Near Tile has a two-way Portal on its Blocked Field. This Portal becomes active only after all Neutral Combats on that Tile have been defeated.
  \item The Center Island features Portals in each Tile's Blocked Field.
  \item Assign a different Resource type to each Center Island Map Tile. When teleporting to the Center Island, roll a Resource Die and teleport to the Map Tile matching the rolled Resource.
  \item Using a portal costs 1 \svg{movement}.
\end{itemize}
