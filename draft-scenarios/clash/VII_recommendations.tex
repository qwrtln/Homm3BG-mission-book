\addscenariosection{2}{Clash Scenario}{Alternative location effects}{\images/logistics.png}

\begin{multicols*}{2}

Variants to use for your Center Fields and other locations in addition or instead of the normal rules or to fill in when Scenario rules do not specify the effect. Choose which one(s) to use before starting your game.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Grail}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item While in possession of the Grail Token, your \svg{hand} Limit is increased by 2.
  \item Search (5) \svg{artifact}.
  \item Increase \svg{gold} income by 2 spaces.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{VII Settlement}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Increase all incomes by 1 space.
  \item Reinforce one \svg{silver} Unit for free and increase 2 incomes by 1 space.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Dragon Utopia}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Recruit one dragon you defeated for free.
  \item Search (5) \svg{artifact} and draw Cards up to your \svg{hand} Limit.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Random Town}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Recruit and Reinforce any Faction Units from Random Town's Faction for discount of 5 \svg{gold} each.
  \item Increase one income by 2 spaces.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Cyclops Stockpile}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Gain 2 Neutral Cyclops Units.
  \item
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Temple of the Sea}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Gain Tome of Water artifact and Search (4) \svg{artifact}.
  \item Gain Water Walk Spell.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Obelisk}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Gain an Empowered Token.
  \item Search (4) \svg{artifact}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Sanctuary}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Gain a Tome of \textit{a chosen School of Spell}.
  \item Choose one \svg{spellpower} from your Discard and take it to your Hand.
\end{itemize}

\end{multicols*}
