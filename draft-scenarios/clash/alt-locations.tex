\addsection[2]{Alternative location effects}{\images/logistics.png}

\begin{multicols*}{2}

Variants to use for your Center Fields and other locations in addition or instead of the normal rules or to fill in when Scenario rules do not specify the effect. Choose which one(s) to use before starting your game.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Grail Token}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Your \svg{hand} Limit is increased by 2.
  \item Once per Round, you may discard 1 Card to gain 3 \svg{gold}.
  \item Search (5) \svg{artifact} when gained.
  \item Increase \svg{gold} income by 2 spaces.
  \item Once per Combat, you may set an Attack Die to a chosen side.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{VII Settlement}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Increase all incomes by 1 space.
  \item Reinforce one \svg{silver} Unit for free and increase 2 incomes by 1 space.
  \item Search(2) the \svg{bronze} Neutral Deck and place a +2 \svg{initiative} Stack Token on that Unit.
  \item Reduce the Reinforcement cost by 2 \svg{gold} while this Settlement is in your possession.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Dragon Utopia}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Draw only Dragons for the Combat. Recruit one dragon you defeated for free.
  \item Search (5) \svg{artifact} and draw Cards up to your \svg{hand} Limit.
  \item Gain Crown of Dragontooth and Necklace of Dragonteeth Artifacts.
  \item Search (2) the Minor and Major Artifact Deck and Search (2) the Relic Artifact Deck twice. Additionally, gain 20 \svg{gold}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Random Town}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Recruit and Reinforce any Faction Units from Random Town's Faction for discount of 5 \svg{gold} each.
  \item Increase one income by 2 spaces.
  \item When Recruiting Units, you may Recruit and Reinforce from that Faction's Units instead. You may deploy only one of each Tier to each Combat.
  \item You may use the Random Town's \svg{building_special_tent} or \svg{building_special_gears} effect once per Round.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Cyclops Stockpile}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Gain 2 Neutral Cyclops Units.
  \item Empower or gain an Empowered Archery Ability Card and Reinforce one \svg{silver} or \svg{bronze} \svg{unit_ranged} Unit for free.
  \item You may Recruit and Stack Neutral Cyclops while Cyclops Stockpile is in your possession.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Temple of the Sea}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Gain Tome of Water artifact and Search (4) \svg{artifact}.
  \item Gain Water Walk Spell.
  \item Draw Cards from the \svg{spellpower} Deck until you draw an Expert Water Spell. Gain that and discard the others.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Obelisk}}
When visiting an Obelisk, choose one effect you have not chosen before.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Remove one Card from your M\&M Deck.
  \item Gain an Empowered Token or Empower one Ability Card from your Hand.
  \item You may use your \svg{building_special_tent} or \svg{building_special_gears} effect once this Round wihtout building them.
  \item Search (4) \svg{artifact}. Place the chosen \svg{artifact} face down on your Hero Card. This Card does not count towards your Hand Limit. After using this Artifact, the Card is removed, not discarded.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Sanctuary}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Choose one \svg{spellpower} from your Discard and take it to your Hand.
  \item +2 \svg{empower} to the next \svg{spellpower} you Cast.
  \item Place one Card from your Discard on top of your Deck. \textit{Visitable once per Faction.}
\end{itemize}

\end{multicols*}
